WELCOME TO EUROSYS

Specialist Security Personnel and Systems

Eurosys provides professional manned guarding and security-system installation for clients who need dependable protection without unnecessary complexity. Our work ranges from day-to-day guarding and mobile response to CCTV, alarms, access control and monitoring.

We have a track record of supporting satisfied clients and a strong operational focus across East Anglia, while remaining able to support wider requirements. Our security operatives are trained and hold the appropriate SIA licences for the roles they perform, and our security-system engineers are trained to complete installations for homes, commercial premises and operational sites.

Every requirement is different. Eurosys can support anything from security for a wedding or private function through to retail, corporate, industrial and integrated security-system requirements. We assess the environment, identify the practical risks and build a proportionate solution around the client.

ADVANCED SECURITY SYSTEMS

Protection Designed Around Your Premises

Eurosys designs security systems around the way a property is actually used. We can combine video surveillance, access control, intruder detection and physical security measures into a practical solution for homes, offices, retail premises, warehouses and other operational environments.

Our approach covers initial assessment, system design, installation and ongoing support. The objective is not simply to install equipment, but to provide useful visibility, controlled access and reliable detection where it matters.
