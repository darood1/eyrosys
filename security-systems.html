SECURITY TECHNOLOGY

Systems designed around real risks

Eurosys designs, installs and supports security systems for residential, commercial and operational environments. Rather than treating each device as a standalone product, we consider how CCTV, access control, alarms, fire detection and physical measures can work together to improve visibility, detection and response.

Solutions can scale from a single property to larger multi-site requirements. We focus on practical system design, professional installation, clear handover and ongoing support.

CCTV Surveillance

Advanced CCTV systems for single premises, multi-site estates and higher-risk environments. Solutions can include high-definition cameras, night vision, secure remote access, recording and analytics-ready capability.

Access Control

Manage who can enter specific areas and when. Options can include keypads, proximity credentials, mobile-enabled entry, visitor management and integration with CCTV and alarms.

Intruder Detection

Wired and wireless detection, internal and perimeter protection, alerting and integration with monitoring or response arrangements.

Fire Alarm Systems

Fire detection and alarm solutions designed around the premises, occupancy and risk profile. We can support new installations, upgrades, servicing and integration with other building security systems.

Physical & Perimeter Security

Barriers, gates and other perimeter measures can be coordinated with electronic systems to strengthen the first layer of site protection.

Maintenance & Monitoring

Planned preventative maintenance, system health checks, fault support and monitoring options help keep security systems operational and effective.

INTEGRATED PROTECTION

Technology and people in harmony

Electronic security is most effective when alerts lead to an appropriate response. CCTV, alarms and access-control events can support on-site personnel, mobile response and remote monitoring, helping clients improve situational awareness and use security resources more efficiently.

Designed to adapt

We can assess existing systems, identify gaps and recommend upgrades or new installations that fit the site, operational needs and future expansion.
